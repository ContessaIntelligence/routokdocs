// FAQ

Pricing questions

Do I need my own AI model?

CodeFlux connects to the model you choose: a local model, or an API key from any provider you already use. Model usage is billed by that provider. Your CodeFlux plan covers the agent features listed above.

Will my plan renew automatically?

No. Plans are prepaid for the period you pick (one month or one year). We never charge you again unless you choose to buy another period, and we send a reminder email before your plan expires.

What happens when Pro expires?

Your account returns to the Free plan. Your projects, history and settings stay on your computer. Pro-only features simply pause until you renew.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If you are not satisfied, you can request a full refund within 7 days of your first purchase. See the Refund Policy for details.

How do I pay, and can I get an invoice?

Available payment methods are shown at checkout, and payment is handled by our licensed payment partner, so we never see or store your card details. Receipts are available in your account, and company invoices can be requested at sales@routok.com.

How do Team and Enterprise plans work?

Contact us with your team size and needs. For Team we set up seats and unified billing. For Enterprise we quote an annual agreement that can include SSO, audit logs, policy controls and private deployment.
